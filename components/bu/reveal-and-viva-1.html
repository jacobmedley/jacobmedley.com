Reveal Clear Aligners

Creative Concepts

Summary:

I collaborated with a talented team of designers and marketers to create a campaign for Reveal Clear Aligners. Our goal was to demonstrate that the choice was clear and that the competition couldn’t compare. I opted for a fun and edgy creative tone for the copy and visuals. It was a privilege to develop engaging visuals for concepts from the brilliant designer Rick Hoyle, who took an intriguing "them vs. us" approach.
