Reveal Aligners

Project Brief:

For the Reveal Clear Aligners campaign, I had the pleasure of working with a dynamic team of designers and marketers. Our objective was to clearly showcase the superiority of Reveal Clear Aligners over the competition. To achieve this, we adopted a fun and edgy creative tone, reflected in both the copy and the visuals.

My role involved developing engaging visuals that aligned with this creative direction. It was a privilege to work on concepts from the brilliant designer Rick Hoyle, who employed a compelling "them vs. us" narrative. This approach effectively highlighted the advantages of Reveal Clear Aligners in a distinctive and memorable way.

The following visuals demonstrate the unique and engaging style we crafted for this campaign, blending fun elements with a clear message of superiority.
