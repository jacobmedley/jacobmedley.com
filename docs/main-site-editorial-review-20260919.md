## Registered proposal, before and after

### Consequential addition: Team Building and Mentorship

Before: no published people-development block. B58 is approved source material but
off-page under B59.

After, approved by Jacob but not yet implemented:

- I walk beside junior staff, asking questions until they can name the next step,
  dependency, or piece they can move now.
- I make authority explicit. People can assess the risk and decide without
  bringing every next step back for permission.
- I stay available and remove blockers beyond their control.
- We review outcomes together. We change the conditions behind mistakes and
  understand good results well enough to repeat them.

Why: the set carries B58's actual coaching mechanism. It does not manufacture a
success story. The short third line gives the card some human rhythm instead of four
equally polished resume bullets.

### Low-risk label correction

Before: `How I approach the work`

After: `How I lead the work`

Why: `How I work` already names the prior section. The proposed label removes a
near-duplicate and makes the transition into leadership, employment, mentorship,
expertise, and tools easier to scan.

## Credibility and promise checks

- No new metric appears in B60, so no numerical claim needed reframing.
- The four mentorship bullets inherit no employer, colleague, or private detail.
- `make authority explicit` does not promise unlimited permission. The next sentence
  keeps judgment tied to risk and decisions.
- `remove blockers beyond their control` states Jacob's responsibility, not a
  guaranteed outcome.
- The people-development copy makes no claim about a mentee's promotion, retention,
  performance, or later role.
- The tools list remains historical. Current proficiency is deliberately unclaimed.
- The review does not certify WCAG conformance. Full WCAG 2.0 A and AA evaluation
  remains an integration-stage task after accepted work lands.
