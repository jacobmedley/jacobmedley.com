# Voice Rules

Split into three files. Load all three.

- `docs/jacob-style.md` for mechanics, prohibitions, anti-AI patterns
- `docs/voice-and-tone.md` for register, personality, the moves, reusable lines
- `docs/data-reporting.md` for how every figure is framed and checked

Style is the constraint set. Voice is the personality inside the constraints.
Data reporting applies to anything containing a figure.
