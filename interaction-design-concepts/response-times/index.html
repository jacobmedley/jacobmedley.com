Explanation: From Jakob Nielsen's "Usability Engineering" (1993):

  • 0.1 second: The limit for users feeling that the system is reacting instantaneously.
  • 1 second: The limit for users' flow of thought to stay uninterrupted, even though they notice the delay.
  • 10 seconds: The limit for keeping users' attention focused on the dialogue. For longer delays, users will want to perform other tasks while waiting for the computer to complete the task.
