The Importance of Response Times in User Experience Design

Understanding response times is crucial for crafting user experiences. In this demonstration, we explore how different response times impact the user. The first set of buttons lacks feedback, while the second set provides it. Starting from a swift 100 milliseconds (ms) and extending up to a lengthy 15 seconds (s), this comparison highlights when feedback becomes necessary and how delays can lead to user frustration. Dive in to see why response times matter and how feedback can enhance user satisfaction.

ms: milliseconds s: seconds

With Feedback

No delay between user action and system response.

ms: milliseconds s: seconds

Explanation: From Jakob Nielsen's "Usability Engineering" (1993):

  • 0.1 : The limit for users feeling that the system is reacting instantaneously.
  • 1 seconds: The limit for users' flow of thought to stay uninterrupted, even though they notice the delay.
  • 10 seconds: The limit for keeping users' attention focused on the dialogue. For longer delays, users will want to perform other tasks while waiting for the computer to complete the task.
