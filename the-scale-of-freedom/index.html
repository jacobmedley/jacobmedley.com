The Scale of Freedom Project

Purpose

"The Scale of Freedom" explores the extensive history and timeline of Black American rights in the Americas, focusing on the staggering length and struggle for freedom, equality, and justice. It highlights the recent generation of people who have reached a point where Black Americans have rights and freedoms approaching those of their white counterparts.

Disclaimer:

This project utilized ChatGPT to assist in identifying important events and milestones in Black history. During our review process, we found several errors when verifying the sources.

We made every effort to verify each historical event, but some inaccuracies may have been missed. Please let us know if you notice any discrepancies or have suggestions for improvement.

Deliberately Designed

The experience was deliberately designed to be cumbersome and long to show the length of time it took to achieve the freedoms and rights that Black Americans have today. We purposefully made it difficult to go through the timeline so that the user can feel the scale of time it took to get to this point.

Key events and milestones are used to educate about the progress and regression that have been battled since before America was even a country.

The Horrible Truth

If we look at the timeline, we can see that since 1619 to today, the most active period for the advancement of freedoms for Black Americans was during the Civil Rights Era (1954-1968). During this time frame, significant legislative changes were made to dismantle segregation and institutionalized racism, leading to greater civil liberties and opportunities for Black Americans.
